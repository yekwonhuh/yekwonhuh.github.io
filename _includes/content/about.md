I am a Securities Quantitative Analytics Associate on the Front Office Equities team at Wells Fargo in New York City. My work focuses on equity derivatives pricing, particularly volatility surface modeling and calibration. I also develop agentic AI tools to support quantitative model development. During my Ph.D., my research spanned statistical modeling, machine learning, and uncertainty quantification.

I earned my Ph.D. in Industrial and Systems Engineering at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://www.wisc.edu/), advised by [Professor Kaibo Liu](https://kaibo.ie.wisc.edu/index.html), and an M.S. in Statistics at UW–Madison. Previously, I earned bachelor's degrees in Business Administration and Statistics at Korea University.
